# ADR 0007 — Datasets: lab-generated pcaps plus one public flow dataset

Status: Proposed (Ayush to accept or change)
Date: 2026-10-18

## Context
Panel will ask for dataset provenance. No unauthorized capture of real users.

## Decision
(a) DNS tunneling pcaps generated by the team in an isolated lab (own machines, tools such as iodine or dnscat2) plus benign DNS from own machines with consent. (b) One public flow dataset (candidate: CIC-IDS2017) for FlowSight; choice confirmed by spike ticket T07. (c) The Paper 1 synthetic generator kept as a baseline. Large files are gitignored and fetched by a script; a licence/citation line per dataset lives in `data/README.md`.

## Consequences
Reproducible, legal, citable. Real-looking tunnel traffic gives CoverTrace honest evaluation beyond synthetic numbers.
